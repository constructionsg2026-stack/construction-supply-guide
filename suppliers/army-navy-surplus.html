Quick Summary

Independent army-navy and military surplus stores are still used by construction workers for durable workwear, boots, rain gear, and inexpensive basic PPE. There is no single national chain that dominates this category.

Coverage

Local / independent stores in most metro areas

Best For

Individual workers buying boots, work pants, jackets, and basic gear locally, often at lower prices than specialty safety houses.

Key Strengths

  • Local and easy to visit
  • Work boots, cargo pants, jackets, and rain gear
  • Sometimes cheaper basic gloves, glasses, and bags
  • Useful when a worker needs gear the same day

Things to Know

Quality and jobsite ratings vary widely. Surplus boots and glasses are not automatically ASTM/OSHA compliant. For required safety-toe, EH, or fall-protection gear, use a dedicated safety supplier or a rated boot retailer such as Red Wing or Boot Barn. Search “army navy store near me” or “surplus store near me” for local options.

Contact

No single national website. Search locally for army-navy, surplus, or tactical/workwear stores.
