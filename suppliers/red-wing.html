Quick Summary

Long-established American work-boot company. Red Wing stores and dealers are a standard stop for construction crews that need safety-toe, EH-rated, waterproof, or slip-resistant boots.

Coverage

National (company stores and dealers across the U.S.)

Best For

Construction workers and crews that need fitted safety footwear rather than catalog-only boots.

Key Strengths

  • Strong reputation for durable work boots
  • Safety-toe, composite-toe, EH, and waterproof options
  • In-store fitting at many locations
  • Widely recognized on jobsites

Things to Know

Confirm the boot is a true work/safety style with the current ASTM rating if the job requires safety toes. Heritage styles are not always jobsite-rated.
