One of the largest electrical distributors in North America. Strong across commercial, industrial, and construction markets with extensive branch coverage and deep inventory.

Best For

Large commercial projects, industrial facilities, data centers, and contractors who need broad product availability and national account support.

Key Strengths

  • Massive inventory and branch network
  • Strong in wire & cable, lighting, power distribution, and automation
  • Good national account programs and project support
  • Reliable for large-volume orders

Things to Know

Wesco is a go-to for bigger jobs. Service quality can vary by branch, so relationships at the local level still matter.
